Our Story

Built on Trust, Grown Through Service

Dhara Enterprise began in 2011 as a small shop in Babra, Gujarat — with a simple goal: bring reliable electronics to the local community at honest prices, with the kind of service a neighbor would give.

Over 13 years, we've grown into one of the area's most trusted electronics retailers, serving thousands of families with fans, lights, kitchen appliances, heaters, coolers, and more.

Every product we stock is hand-picked for quality, and every sale comes with the confidence of genuine warranty support.

  • Genuine, brand-verified products only
  • Official warranty on all items
  • Same-day local delivery in Babra
  • Expert installation & repair service
  • Honest pricing — no hidden charges
